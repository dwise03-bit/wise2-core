# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Automotive detailers, recon companies, mobile detail businesses, dealership recon teams, and vehicle service businesses. Primary field users are technicians working around vehicles with one hand, variable connectivity, glare, gloves, and time pressure. Owners, managers, and office staff use the same system for customer records, scheduling, revenue, and oversight.

The core job is to scan or enter a vehicle, retrieve or create its permanent record, document the work, complete a checklist, record payment, and preserve the full customer and vehicle history without switching apps.

## Product Purpose

CJAYS Auto Recon Solutions is the business-owned operating system for auto recon and detailing. It connects Customer → Vehicle → Job → Services → Photos → Documents → Payments → History, optimized around the field workflow: Open → Scan VIN → Find/Create Vehicle → Customer → Start Job → Before Photos → Checklist → Work → After Photos → Payment → Complete.

Success means a technician can complete that workflow quickly on a real Android phone, continue through weak connectivity, and retrieve the record later in seconds without losing or fabricating data.

## Brand Personality

Premium, capable, direct. The voice is concise and professional, with the confidence of a well-kept workshop and the precision of an automotive instrument cluster. The product avoids hype and tells users exactly what happened, what is pending, and what needs attention.

## Anti-references

- Uncustomized generic Material styling
- Decorative glass, excessive rounding, nested cards, and novelty interactions
- Fake production metrics or integrations that are not configured
- Tiny controls, low-contrast gray text, and layouts designed for a desk instead of a service bay
- Consumer-car-app styling that prioritizes spectacle over operational clarity

## Design Principles

1. Scan is the center of gravity: the primary action remains obvious from every main screen.
2. Preserve field data: offline work is explicit, queued, conflict-aware, and never silently discarded.
3. Show operational truth: empty, loading, permission, sync, and error states are honest and actionable.
4. One-handed in the bay: large targets, short labels, predictable navigation, and high contrast.
5. Vehicle history compounds: every job strengthens a permanent customer and vehicle record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA-equivalent contrast and Android accessibility guidance. Touch targets are at least 48dp. Information is never conveyed by color alone. Support screen readers, font scaling, reduced motion, clear permission explanations, and layouts from compact phones through practical tablet widths.
