# Cinematic Handoff QA Checklist

- [ ] Brand DNA reflects supplied facts; assumptions are labeled; no unsupported claims exist.
- [ ] No crowns or crown-like motif appears without an explicit project-specific request.
- [ ] Every scene has a narrative job and shares a coherent environment/material/lighting language.
- [ ] Hero includes subject, composition, desktop copy zone, mobile reframe, CTA placement, and motion opportunity.
- [ ] Each asset has ID, priority, purpose, ratio, resolution, variant, method, and dependency.
- [ ] Prompts include subject, environment, composition, camera, light, materials, depth, ratio, and negatives.
- [ ] UI behavior, panel material, contrast, focus, and normal controls are documented.
- [ ] Desktop, tablet, and 9:16 mobile have deliberate crop rules; important content survives.
- [ ] Motion has a narrative purpose, static fallback, and reduced-motion treatment.
- [ ] Media strategy covers poster/fallback, loading, dimensions, and compression.
- [ ] Next build action, locked decisions, and assumptions are unambiguous.
