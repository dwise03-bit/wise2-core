# Visual Standard

## Fixed production bar

Primary scenes are 4K-ready hyper-realistic cinematic photography or rendering. They use physically credible material response, motivated lighting, atmospheric depth, real reflections, disciplined contrast, clear focal hierarchy, and distinct foreground/midground/background planes. Design every hero as a web composition with intentional negative space.

## Adaptive brand layer

Choose the world from the business, audience, and conversion task—not a preselected “futuristic” look. A packaging supplier may earn a botanical product world; a heritage streetwear brand may use editorial grit and period cues; a logistics brand may use fleet scale and conversion-safe proof surfaces. A technology brand is not automatically neon or cyberpunk.

## Quality gate

Every primary visual must answer: What is happening? Why does it exist in this story? Where will copy and controls live? What survives in 9:16? Does it look like the same world as adjacent scenes? Is the material and light plausible?

## Global exclusion

No crowns, crown icons, crown-shaped marks, crown graffiti, crown watermarks, or crown-like ornament unless explicitly requested for that specific project/visual. Also avoid generic AI gloss, random particles, meaningless floating UI, generic SaaS dashboards, embedded essential text, impossible surfaces, oversaturation, and decorative clutter.
