# Motion and Responsive Art Direction

## Motion budget

Assign motion by narrative value: ambient atmosphere (very subtle), a hero state change, a section transition, product/character behavior, and interaction feedback. Use opacity and transform first; keep loops short, quiet, and optional. State trigger, duration, easing feel, loop endpoint, fallback still, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Video handoff

For a video prompt specify start frame, end frame, camera path, subject motion, environmental motion, duration, loop seam, and transition role. Prefer a controlled 4–8 second loop where a still cannot communicate the experience. Never use autoplay audio by default.

## Crop rules

Create separate art direction for desktop (usually 16:9 or wider), tablet (4:3/3:2), and mobile (9:16). Declare the protected subject region and headline/UI safe zone as percentage coordinates. On mobile, reframe or use a dedicated asset; do not center-crop a wide scene blindly. Keep conversion controls in normal document flow even when a scene is full bleed.

## Performance

Plan a poster image, modern compressed media formats, explicit dimensions, responsive sources, lazy loading below the fold, and a static fallback. Motion should not be required for comprehension or conversion.
