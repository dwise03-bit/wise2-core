# Materials, Lighting, and UI Integration

## Material system

Choose 2–4 hero materials with believable age, grain, roughness, seams, patina, and reflections. Match them to the business: brushed steel and durable paint for logistics; linen, lace, and warm metals for intimate apparel; molded biodegradable fiber and glass for premium packaging. Include a surface token for panels, borders, controls, and hover states.

## Lighting system

Define key, fill, practicals, accent, shadow character, and color-temperature relationship. Restrict the palette to one dominant temperature plus a purposeful accent. Preserve enough contrast for text and controls; do not make darkness an excuse for unreadability.

## Environment-bound UI

UI is a spatial resident: its material, edge treatment, reflection, shadow, and glow intensity should answer to the scene. Use semantically normal navigation, controls, form labels, focus rings, and readable hierarchy. Avoid floating decorative panels with no relation to light, perspective, or task.

## Accessibility baseline

Meet normal contrast targets, expose keyboard focus, use real headings and buttons, avoid image-only messaging, offer reduced motion, and prevent video/animation from obscuring form completion or reading.
