# Cherry Count™ — Demo Walkthrough

**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Audience:** Client (non-technical)  
**Goal:** Build excitement and confidence

---

## 1. Landing (1 min)
- Open Cherry Count app
- Show brand: "Track It. Pack It. Profit."
- Note: "Powered by WISE² — enterprise tech, beautifully hidden"

## 2. Dashboard (2 min)
- Greeting: "Hey Boss 💋"
- Point out: Today's Sales, Inventory count, Low Stock alerts
- Show Next Pop-Up card
- Highlight Smart Insight from Cherry AI

## 3. Inventory (3 min)
- Browse product list with photos and variants
- Open Cherry Bomb Hoodie detail
- Show size/color variants, bin locations, profit margin
- Point out low-stock warning on Lavender Crop Top

## 4. Pop-Ups (3 min)
- Show Downtown Night Market event
- Packing progress bar (60% packed)
- Open Pop-Up Mode — live sales, checklist, bin locations
- Show Pack Smart — scan containers, see contents

## 5. Sales & Customers (2 min)
- Sales dashboard: revenue, profit, best sellers
- Customer profile: Brianna R. with "Need Medium" demand signal
- Explain: "Cherry Count learns what your customers actually want"

## 6. Cherry AI (2 min)
- Show daily briefing and restock suggestion
- "Your smart business partner that works 24/7"
- Emphasize: AI suggests, you decide

## 7. Wrap-Up (2 min)
- Show client presentation slide 14 (deliverables)
- Walk through next steps (slide 15)
- Q&A

---

## Key Talking Points

- "Built specifically for YOUR business"
- "One system, one login, one beautiful experience"
- "Works on your phone at the pop-up"
- "Never wonder what you forgot to pack"
- "Know what's making you money, in real time"
