# WISE² Women's Concealed Carry VR Coaching — One-Pager

## The Opportunity

**Market**: Women 25–55 seeking personal concealed-carry coaching (21M CCW permit holders in USA; ~40% female = 8.4M TAM)

**Problem**: Firearms training is male-dominated, intimidating for women, generic (no personalization), expensive ($500–$2K for live courses)

**Solution**: 1-on-1 VR coaching platform with female instructors, personalized drill progression, and community support

**Differentiation vs. ACE VR**: They do solo sport drills; you do personalized defensive training with real coaches

---

## Development Plan

### **Phase 1: MVP (16 weeks, $150K–$200K)**
- Draw drills (all 6 positions including purse/bra carry)
- Accuracy drills (7/15/25 yards, moving targets)
- Stress scenarios (time pressure, audio threats)
- Coaching interface (real-time observation, voice feedback, instant replay)
- Progression system (White → Black belt)
- Analytics & messaging platform

### **Phase 2: Scale (Q2 2027)**
- Expand instructor network (20+ coaches)
- Multiplayer scenarios (peer training)
- Video library (pre-recorded tips)
- Enterprise onboarding (academy partnerships)

### **Phase 3: Adjacent Markets (Q3 2027+)**
- Rifle/shotgun modules
- Law enforcement vertical
- Tactical movement (room clearing)
- De-escalation scenarios

## Execution Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Hand-tracking latency affects draw timing | Medium | Early prototype; fallback to controller input |
| Instructor recruitment is hard | Low | Passive income appeal; coaches earn $700–$1,400/mo |
| Women don't adopt VR for firearms | Low | Market research shows high demand (r/CCW_Women, FB groups) |
| Meta policy changes restrict app | Low | Build on App Lab first; monitor HMS policy |
| Competition from ACE (branching into coaching) | Medium | Lock in early adopters with community; brand women-first |
