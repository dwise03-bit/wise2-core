# WISE² Women's Concealed Carry Coaching — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Positioning**: "Your personal concealed-carry coach in VR — designed by women, for women"

**Target Customer**: Women 25–55, concealed-carry permit holders, seeking **personalized, judgment-free training**

---

## Phase 1: Beta Launch (Month 1–3)

### **Recruit Students (50–100)**

#### **Channel 1: Women's Concealed Carry Communities**
- **Subreddit**: r/Concealed_Carry (monitor posts from women)
- **Facebook Groups**:
  - "Women Concealed Carry"
  - "Lady Shooters"
  - "Women Firearm Enthusiasts"
- **Strategy**: Post with testimonials from beta instructors, not promotional spam
- **Message**: "Join our closed beta: 1-on-1 coaching from a female instructor in VR"
- **Incentive**: First 50 students get Intro Program ($597) for $299 (50% off beta launch)

#### **Channel 2: Micro-Influencers**
- Partner with 5–10 women's firearm YouTube creators / Instagram accounts
- Offer **free Intro Program + handset** for honest review
- Target: 500K–2M combined followers in women's firearms niche

#### **Channel 3: Firearms Training Academies**
- Pitch to 10–15 female-owned or female-friendly ranges
- Offer **white-label referral** (range gets 20% commission for each student referred)
- Plaque on their wall: "Try WISE² Women's VR Coaching Here"

#### **Channel 4: CCW Permit Classes**
- Target women taking concealed-carry classes
- Sponsor 2–3 local permit courses
- Offer beta access to course graduates

### **Recruit Instructors (5–10)**

#### **Instructor Profile**
- Licensed, certified firearms trainer (minimum 5 years experience)
- Female (critical for trust)
- Interested in passive/semi-passive income ($700–$1,400/month per 5–10 students)
- Comfortable with VR technology (2-hour onboarding required)

#### **Recruitment Channels**
- **LinkedIn**: Post "Hiring Female Firearms Instructors for VR Platform"
- **Firearms Instructor Networks**: IADCFT, NRA Instructor Council
- **Facebook Groups**: Female firearms instructors, trainer communities
- **Direct Outreach**: Email 20–30 known female instructors in top metro areas

#### **Pitch to Instructors**
> "Teach concealed-carry to women online, on your schedule. Earn $700–$1,400/month passive income per 5–10 students. Platform handles tech, billing, scheduling. You focus on coaching."

---

## Phase 2: Monetization & Scaling (Month 4–6)

### **Student Acquisition**
- **Retention Focus**: Get first cohort to complete Intro Program (12 weeks)
- **Testimonials**: Collect success stories, before/after accuracy metrics
- **Referral Program**: $50/student referral bonus (students + coaches)
- **Cohort-Based Approach**: Run 2–3 cohort launches/month (students progress together, build community)

### **Instructor Growth**
- **Tier System**: Incentivize excellence
  - Tier 1 (New): 70/30 split
  - Tier 2 (10+ grads): 80/20 split (or 100% if proven track record)
- **Batch Coaching**: Allow 1 instructor to coach 2–3 students simultaneously (efficiency)
- **Training Library**: Pre-record video tips instructors can send to students (reduce coaching burden)

### **Community Building**
- **Private Discord**: Connect all students + instructors
- **Monthly Challenges**: "Quick-Draw Challenge" (all students compete for virtual badge)
- **Testimonial Wall**: Showcase graduate transformations (accuracy before/after)
- **Accountability Groups**: Pair students for buddy check-ins between sessions

## Marketing Messaging (How to Talk About It)

### **For Students**
> **"Train at your own pace with a female coach who gets you."**  
> No judgment. No crowded ranges. No male-dominated atmosphere. Just you, your VR coach, and real progress.

### **For Instructors**
> **"Earn passive income teaching what you love."**  
> Reach women who'd never walk into a range. Earn $700–$1,400/month per 5–10 students. No scheduling stress—students book coaches in the app.

### **Key Messaging Pillars**
1. **Safe Space for Women**: Only female instructors, women-only cohorts, no judgment
2. **Personalized Coaching**: Not AI feedback, not generic drills—real instructor guidance
3. **Progress Tracking**: See measurable improvement (draw time, accuracy, confidence)
4. **Flexibility**: Train in your home, on your schedule, pause anytime
5. **Community**: Join women who are serious about concealed carry
