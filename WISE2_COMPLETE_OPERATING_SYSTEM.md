## 🔗 The 8 Engines

### 1. Commerce Accelerator
**Purpose**: Build complete ecommerce businesses in 5-7 days

**6 AI Agents**:
- Market Intelligence → Trends, demand, TAM
- Competitor Intelligence → Positioning, pricing
- Customer Psychology → Avatars, triggers, messaging
- Brand Architect → Identity, colors, positioning
- Product Validator → Viability, margins
- Store Builder → Store architecture

**9 Business Models**:
- Subscription, Dropshipping, POD, Digital, Hybrid, Wholesale, Private Label, Service, AI-Generated

**Workflow**:
```
Client Questionnaire (4 questions)
    ↓
6 AI Agents research in parallel
    ↓
All use Model Orchestrator for intelligence
    ↓
Complete business ready in 5-7 days
(brand, store, marketing, automations, analytics)
```

### 2. Model Orchestrator
**Purpose**: Intelligent AI model routing (minimum cost, maximum quality)

**13 Supported Models**:
- **Tier 1 Premium**: Claude Opus 5, GPT-4 Turbo, Gemini 2.0 Pro
- **Tier 2 Free**: DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, Nemotron, + 5 more

**6+ Providers**:
- Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, GitHub, NVIDIA

**Intelligent Routing**:
```
Task Context (complexity, cost, latency, quality)
    ↓
Score all available models
    ↓
Select best model + fallback
    ↓
Auto-switch on failure
    ↓
Track cost & performance
```

**Cost Savings**:
- Simple tasks: 99.7% cheaper (free models)
- Complex tasks: Premium quality when needed
- Annual (1M tasks): ~$300K saved

### 3. Content Engine
**Purpose**: Auto-generate all marketing content

**Content Types**:
- Landing pages (hero, problem, solution, CTA)
- Sales pages (agitation, objection handling, guarantee)
- Email sequences (5-email campaigns per offer)
- Blog articles (SEO-optimized, 1000-2000 words)
- Product descriptions (benefit-focused, social proof)
- FAQ pages (objection addressing)

**Psychology Integration**:
- Emotional triggers embedded
- Objection handling included
- Conversion-focused CTAs
- Mobile-responsive structure

**API Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/wise2/content/landing-page`

### 4. Creative Engine
**Purpose**: Generate visual content prompts

**Capabilities**:
- Product image prompts (Midjourney/DALL-E format)
- Ad creative (headlines, body, CTA, visual brief)
- Video scripts (platform-specific: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram)
- UGC-style content
- WISE² cinematic branding integration

**Output**: Ready-to-use prompts for AI image/video generation

**API Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/wise2/creative/product-image`

### 5. Marketing Engine
**Purpose**: Strategic multi-channel campaigns

**Capabilities**:
- Channel mix optimization (FB, TikTok, Pinterest, Google, YouTube, Email, SMS)
- Budget allocation strategy
- Expected ROAS projection (3x+)

- A/B test matrix
- Retargeting strategy

**Output**: Complete marketing strategy with spend allocation

**API Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/wise2/marketing/campaign-strategy`

### 6. Automation Engine
**Purpose**: Orchestrate business workflows

**Automation Types**:
- Email sequences (triggered by actions)
- SMS campaigns (urgency-driven)
- Inventory syncing (real-time)
- Customer support (AI chatbot)
- Upsells & cross-sells (post-purchase)

**Output**: Step-by-step workflow with conditions, delays, error handling

**API Endpoint**: `POST /api/v1/wise2/automation/workflow`

### 7. Analytics Engine
**Purpose**: Track KPIs and generate recommendations

**Tracks**:
- Revenue (total, monthly, recurring)

- Customer metrics (CAC, LTV, retention, churn)
- Marketing metrics (ROAS, conversion rate, AOV)

**Calculates**:
- Health Score (0-100)

- Forecast (revenue, churn)

**API Endpoints**:
- `POST /api/v1/wise2/analytics/recommendations`
- `POST /api/v1/wise2/analytics/health-score`

### 8. Knowledge Loop
**Purpose**: Learn from successful campaigns

**Records**:

- `GET /api/v1/wise2/knowledge/top-performers`

## 🔄 Complete Workflow Example

**Scenario**: Launch an ecommerce subscription box business

### Step 1: Client Questionnaire
```
User answers 4 questions:
- Business model: Subscription
- Target audience: Busy professionals
- Budget: $5,000
- Timeline: 3 weeks
```

### Step 2: Commerce Accelerator Researches
```
6 agents run in parallel:
- Market Intelligence: Research subscription box market
- Competitor: Analyze 10 competitors
- Psychology: Create avatars for professionals
- Brand: Design brand identity
- Product: Validate product viability
- Store: Design store architecture
```

### Step 3: Each Agent Uses Model Orchestrator
```
Agent needs to research market trends
    → Model Orchestrator routes to cheapest suitable model
    → DeepSeek Coder for research (99.7% cheaper)
    → Fallback to Qwen if needed
    → Tracks cost ($0.0001/1k tokens)
```

### Step 4: Content Engine Generates Copy
```
Landing page:
- Product descriptions
- Email sequences (5 emails)
- Blog articles (SEO)
- FAQ (objection handling)
```

### Step 5: Creative Engine Generates Visuals
```
Product images:
- Hero image prompt
- Lifestyle images (3)
- Ad creatives (5 variations)
- Video script (30-second)
```

### Step 6: Marketing Engine Creates Strategy
```
Campaign strategy:
- Facebook: $1,500 (audience targeting)
- TikTok: $1,250 (viral content)
- Email: $750 (nurture)
- Pinterest: $1,000 (high intent)
- Retargeting: $500 (conversion)
- Expected ROAS: 3.5x
```

### Step 7: Automation Engine Sets Up Workflows
```
Automations:
- Welcome sequence (triggered on signup)
- Cart abandonment (if cart unpurchased)
- Post-purchase upsell (after 7 days)
- Retention campaign (monthly)
- Churn prevention (if no purchase in 60 days)
```

### Step 8: Analytics Engine Tracks Performance
```
Live dashboards:
- Revenue: $0 → $5K → $15K (projecting $50K month 2)
- CAC: $145
- LTV: $420
- Health Score: 82/100
- Recommendations: Increase email frequency, test CTA color
```

### Step 9: Knowledge Loop Learns
```
Recording:
- "Subject: Busy professionals" ROAS 4.2x
- "Headline: Save 10 hours/week" converts 8.2%
- "Audience: 35-45, managers" retention 85%
- Insight: Professionals respond to time-saving angle
```

**Result**: Complete business launched, generating revenue, continuously improving
