# Brand guide

- Product name: **WISE² SOUND LABS**
- Supporting line: CREATE × COLLABORATE × PERFORM
- Foundation: near-black, dark navy, gunmetal, smoked glass
- Accents: electric cyan, neon green, restrained blue, selective crown gold
- Alerts: recording red and warning orange only
- Use the superscript squared identity (`WISE²`) in visible UI; use `WISE2` only for package names and filesystem compatibility.
- Keep glow disciplined: edges and active states may glow, while body copy remains high contrast and readable.
