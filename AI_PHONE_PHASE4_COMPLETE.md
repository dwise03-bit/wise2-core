## 🎵 Audio Intelligence System

### Tone Analysis

```typescript
ToneAnalyzer
├── analyzeTone(audioBuffer) → ToneMetrics
│   ├── Pitch variation (monotone vs. expressive)
│   ├── Speed variation (consistent vs. jerky)
│   ├── Volume variation (energetic vs. flat)
│   ├── Stress indicators (high pitch, faster tempo)
│   └── Confidence indicators (steady, lower pitch)
│
├── emotionFromTone(toneMetrics) → EmotionScore
│   ├── Happy: Rising pitch, faster, varied tempo
│   ├── Frustrated: Higher pitch, jerky speed, volume spike
│   ├── Sad: Lower pitch, slower, monotone
│   ├── Confused: Question-like pitch rises
│   └── Confident: Steady, measured
│
├── stressLevel(audioBuffer) → StressScore (0-10)
│   ├── High pitch + faster speech = stress
│   ├── Irregular breathing patterns = anxiety
│   ├── Voice strain = urgency
│   └── Confidence: 88%
│
└── urgencyIndicator(audioBuffer) → UrgencyLevel
    ├── High: Urgent action needed
    ├── Medium: Important but not critical
    └── Low: Routine inquiry
```
