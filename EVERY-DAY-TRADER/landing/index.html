    THE METHOD

    The Road Has a System

    Knowledge builds the route. Discipline keeps you in your lane.

    THE ROUTE

    From Chaos to Process

    1. 1

      Observe

      Watch the flow. Note what is happening before deciding what it means.

    2. 2

      Plan

      Define the thesis, the stop, and the size in writing.

    3. 3

      Practice

      Run the plan on paper. Follow the rules even when it is uncomfortable.

    4. 4

      Review

      Compare outcomes to the plan. Keep what worked; fix what did not.
