AUTOMATION CAPABILITIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer Journey Automation:
  1. Visitor → /pricing
     └─ Analytics: track page view & plan interest

  2. Select Plan → /checkout
     └─ Analytics: track checkout start

  3. Complete Payment
     └─ Billing: create subscription in Stripe & database
     └─ Workspace: provision workspace
     └─ Email: send welcome email
     └─ Analytics: track payment success

  4. Auto-redirect → /onboarding
     └─ UI: guide through 5-step wizard
     └─ Email: day 1, 3, 7 onboarding tips (scheduled)

  5. Complete Onboarding
     └─ Database: mark onboarding complete
     └─ Analytics: track engagement

  6. Monthly Billing
     └─ Stripe: charge subscription
     └─ Database: update invoice
     └─ Email: send invoice
     └─ Analytics: track MRR

  7. Plan Change (Upgrade/Downgrade)
     └─ Stripe: update subscription & handle proration
     └─ Database: log change
     └─ Email: send confirmation
     └─ Analytics: track ARPU

  8. Payment Failure (3x Retry)
     └─ Attempt 1: Email warning (soft)
     └─ Attempt 2: Email warning (medium)
     └─ Attempt 3: Email warning (urgent) + suspend access
     └─ Update: change subscription status to past_due
     └─ Analytics: track payment failures

  9. Cancellation
     └─ Database: set cancel_at_period_end = true
     └─ Email: send cancellation confirmation + retention offer
     └─ Analytics: track churn reason
     └─ Schedule: win-back email for 30 days later

  10. Post-Cancellation (30+ Days)
      └─ Email: win-back campaign with 30% discount
      └─ Analytics: track win-back effectiveness
