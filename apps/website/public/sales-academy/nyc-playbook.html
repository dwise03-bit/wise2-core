📚 NYC Service Playbook

Step-by-step guide to delivering WISE² to NYC businesses

Sales Process: 4-Week Deal Flow

Week 1: Discovery
Understand Their Current State
Goals: Identify pain, establish credibility, uncover decision-makers

• Discovery call: 30 minutes (operations, dispatch, tech stack)
• Site visit: Walk through office, observe dispatch process
• Interview: Tech lead or senior tech about field frustrations
• Document: Current workflow, systems, headcount, revenue

Deliverable: Written summary of findings & initial recommendations
✅ OUTCOME: Clear understanding of their operation. Are they a fit? Proceed to audit proposal.
Week 2: Audit & Proposal
Quantify the Opportunity
Actions: Deep assessment, ROI modeling, tailored proposal

• Operations Audit: Detailed workflow assessment (4-6 hours)
• Metric modeling: Calculate their specific ROI (3x, 4-week payback, etc.)
• Proposal: 15-page doc with findings, recommendations, investment
• Demo: Live walkthrough of WISE² Command with their data

Investment Range: Audit tier $3.5K-6K (included in full engagement)
✅ OUTCOME: Signed audit. Executive sponsor and tech sponsor aligned on vision.
Week 3: Kickoff & Technical Setup
Implementation Begins
Actions: Integration, data migration, team training

• Kickoff: Executive alignment meeting (2 hours)
• Systems integration: Connect to QuickBooks, Salesforce, phone
• Data: Import 6 months of historical jobs, customer data
• Training: 2-hour team workshop (office + field)
• Go-live: Pick one module to start (usually AI Phone or Command)

Investment Range: Launch tier $10K-18K
✅ OUTCOME: First module live. Team training complete. Early wins captured.
Week 4: Scaling
Expansion & Optimization
Actions: Add modules based on early wins

• Measure: ROI on first module (calls recovered, jobs added)
• Expand: Add Field Tech, Advanced Analytics based on impact
• Optimize: Workflows refined based on 3-week data
• Upsell: Present Grow/Automate tiers for next phase

Ongoing: Monthly check-ins, quarterly business reviews
✅ OUTCOME: Multi-module deployment path clear. Customer is WISE² advocate.

Key Positioning for NC Market

✅ What Resonates

  • 🎯 "Scale without hiring" - NYC businesses fear talent shortage more than anything
  • 📞 "Never miss a call again" - Phone system solves their #1 operational pain
  • ⚙️ "One operating system" - They're tired of managing 4+ disconnected tools
  • 📊 "Make decisions faster" - Real-time data matters to growth-minded leaders

❌ What Doesn't Land

  • Technical jargon - They care about business results, not architecture
  • Long implementation timelines - NYC businesses value fast time-to-value
  • Enterprise features they don't need - Keep it simple and practical

Common Deal Structures

Small Contractor ($500K-2M revenue)

• Audit ($5K) → Launch ($12K) → Grow ($18K) over 4 months
• Total investment: $35K
• ROI: 400%+ first year
• Decision maker: Owner or VP Ops

Mid-Market Contractor ($2M-10M revenue)

• Audit ($6K) → Launch ($18K) → Grow + Automate ($45K) over 6 months
• Total investment: $69K
• ROI: 300%+ first year
• Decision maker: CFO + VP Ops

Large Contractor ($10M+ revenue)

• Custom assessment → Full platform deployment → Dominate tier
• Investment: $100K+
• ROI: 250%+ (absolute dollars matter more)
• Decision maker: C-suite + Operations committee
