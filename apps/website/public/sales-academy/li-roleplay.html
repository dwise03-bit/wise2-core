🎯 Long Island Role-Plays & Certification

Live training scenarios and sales certification for WISE² team

Role-Play Scenarios

Scenario 1: The Skeptical Owner
Profile: 10-person HVAC contractor. Been running the same way for 15 years. Resistant to change.

Challenge: Owner says "We've tried new systems before. They always fail. I don't have time for this."

Your job: Acknowledge their past pain, establish credibility, propose a low-risk trial.

Success metric: Get a 30-day trial agreed on AI Phone module only.
✓ Empathy for past failures
✓ Proof that WISE² is different
✓ Risk-reversal (trial, money-back guarantee)
✓ Starting small (one module)
Scenario 2: The Tech-Savvy CFO
Profile: $5M electrical contractor. CFO is very analytical, wants data.

Challenge: CFO asks: "How do I know these ROI numbers are real? What if we don't recover 20 calls a month?"

Your job: Provide data-driven proof, show how to measure, offer implementation success guarantees.

Success metric: CFO agrees to full ROI audit with measurement framework.
✓ Data and metrics confidence
✓ Measurement framework (how to track)
✓ Conservative vs. aggressive ROI scenarios
✓ Performance guarantees
Scenario 3: The Dispatcher Blocker
Profile: 15-person contractor. Dispatcher (15 years at company) is blocking the tech adoption.

Challenge: Dispatcher fears job security: "This will automate me out."

Your job: Reframe the role, show how they become more valuable, get them on board.

Success metric: Dispatcher becomes an internal champion for the rollout.
✓ Stakeholder mapping (who's the real blocker)
✓ Fear/job security handling
✓ Reframing automation as expansion
✓ Building internal champions
Scenario 4: The Budget Squeeze
Profile: $3M contractor. Owner interested but "cash is tight right now."

Challenge: They want the solution but can't afford the upfront cost.

Your job: Show ROI fast enough to justify cash spend, or structure a payment plan.

Success metric: Agreement on payment terms or fast-ROI trial path.
✓ Creative pricing structures
✓ ROI payback communication
✓ Phase-based rollout (spread cost)
✓ Financing options
