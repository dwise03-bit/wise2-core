NYC Market Context

NYC businesses are different: They move fast, they're sophisticated, they've seen every pitch. They value speed, data, and ROI clarity. They're not interested in "maybe" — they want to know exactly what success looks like and when they'll see results.

Key NYC Dynamics

  • Higher deal values ($15K–$60K+ vs. national average)
  • Faster sales cycles (30–60 days vs. 60–90)
  • Multiple decision-makers (legal, finance, operations)
  • Emphasis on integration with existing systems (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.)
  • Custom solutions are standard, not exception

NYC Industries We Serve

WISE² works with:

  • • Financial Services & FinTech: Trading desks, wealth management, crypto platforms (need fast infrastructure)
  • • Tech Startups: Series A–C companies needing to scale operations without adding headcount
  • • Agencies & Consulting: Marketing, PR, design firms needing client management + project automation
  • • Real Estate & PropTech: Brokers, developers, management companies scaling operations
  • • E-commerce & D2C: Online retailers needing CRM, marketing automation, customer intelligence
  • • Professional Services: Law, accounting, consulting firms modernizing client delivery
  • • Hospitality & F&B: Restaurant groups, hotels, event companies managing operations at scale

NYC Pricing Reality

NYC customers expect premium pricing for premium service. Unlike regional markets, they're not hunting for discounts — they want proven ROI.

Typical NYC Deal Values

  • Audit: $5K–$8K (more complex businesses)
  • Launch: $15K–$30K (higher expectations for brand/design)
  • Grow: $18K–$35K (multi-channel marketing expected)
  • Automate: $30K–$75K (AI phone + integrations required)
  • Dominate: $50K–$200K+ (custom build, ongoing)
