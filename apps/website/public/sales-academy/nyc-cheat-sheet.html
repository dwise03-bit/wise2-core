⏱️ Discovery Call Flow (45 min for NYC)

0-3 min: GREET
"Hi [name], this is [you] from WISE². I work with [industry] companies in NYC on [specific outcome]. Quick question: are you dealing with [specific problem] right now?"
3-20 min: AUDIT
"Tell me about your current situation. What's working? What's costing you time or money? If you could snap your fingers and fix one thing, what would it be?"
20-35 min: REVIEW
"Based on what you've told me, most [industry] companies face [problem]. I've worked with companies like [similar company] who solved this in [timeframe]. Here's how:"
35-45 min: CLOSE
"For your situation, I'd recommend starting with [Phase]. This would position you to [specific outcome]. Are you open to exploring this further?"

🚧 NYC Objection Responses

"We're already using [Salesforce/HubSpot/other]"
"Totally fair. Here's what I hear from companies like yours: they have the tool, but the workflow isn't optimized for your specific operation. We specialize in taking that tool and building the automation + intelligence layer that makes it actually work for your team. Want to see how that works?"
"Price is too high / We don't have budget"
"I understand. Let me ask: if we could save your team 15+ hours per week on admin work—what's that worth? At $75/hr loaded cost, that's $1,200/week, $60K/year. Most of our clients recoup investment in first project. Sound reasonable?"
"This is too complex / Our business is too unique"
"That's actually why we exist. Off-the-shelf tools don't work for companies like yours. That's the whole point of the 'Dominate' tier—we build custom systems. We've done this with [similar company]. Let's explore what's actually needed."
"Let me think about it / We'll get back to you"
"Of course. What specifically do you want to think through? [Listen]. Here's the thing: for every month you delay, you're leaving $[calculate monthly impact] on the table. How about we schedule a follow-up for [specific day] to discuss further?"
"Can you send me information?"
"Happy to. But here's what usually happens: you read it, it makes sense, and then it sits in your inbox. What if instead, I send it to you, and we hop on a call [specific time] so I can answer your questions directly?"

⚡ NYC Sales Rules

✓ Move fast: NYC people respect speed. If you say "next Friday," be ready Tuesday.
✗ Never BS: They'll catch it. Admit what you don't know, offer to get back to them.
✓ Show ROI math: They want to see the calculation, not a feeling.
✗ Don't oversell: Start small (Audit), prove it, then grow.
✓ Log EVERYTHING: CRM is law. Especially in NYC where deals move fast.
✗ Don't invent scope: Escalate custom asks to Daniel. Now.
