📋 NYC Sales Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for selling WISE² in New York City

The NC Opportunity

5,000+ tech companies $5B+ annual spend Low tech adoption Growth-hungry

NC has more HVAC, electrical, and service contractors than any state in the region. They're growing but still managing operations in spreadsheets and Outlook. High pain, high motivation to solve.

90-Second Pitch

OPENING:
"I've been working with contractors across New York City. The ones who are winning have one thing in common: they've moved from managing in spreadsheets to having one operating system that connects dispatch, customer communication, and field data."
PROBLEM:
"Most teams lose 5-10 jobs a week to poor coordination. Miss calls, slow dispatch, techs waiting for information, office staff manually routing everything."
SOLUTION:
"WISE² Command is your operating system. One dashboard. Real-time job tracking. AI phone that answers 24/7. Field tech app that syncs instantly."
OUTCOME:
"Typical result: 2-3 extra jobs per tech per week, 20-30 recovered calls monthly, and you don't need to hire more admin staff. That's $100K-200K in new revenue on the same headcount."

Objection Handlers

❌ "It's too expensive"

"I get that. But a 10-person team misses 20 calls a month. If 5% turn into jobs, that's $6K in lost revenue monthly. WISE² costs $1,500/month. You're ahead in week one."

❌ "We already have a system"

"Which systems? Most teams use 3-4 that don't talk to each other. WISE² connects them all. Your Outlook, QuickBooks, and phone operating as one operating system."

❌ "We're fine with spreadsheets"

"When you grow to 15-20 techs, spreadsheets become your growth ceiling. WISE² scales without adding staff. Plus, real-time data beats anything a spreadsheet can do."

❌ "We need to think about it"

"Let me show you a 20-minute walkthrough with your office manager and one tech. You'll see live how this works. Then you decide. Does Tuesday work?"

Key Metrics to Lead With

ROI 3-4x first year — Payback in 4-8 weeks
SCALE +3 jobs/tech/week — No additional hiring
CALLS 20-30 recovered/month — 24/7 answering
ADMIN 15+ hours saved/week — Dispatcher becomes profit center
