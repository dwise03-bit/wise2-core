🎯 Common NC Objections

Objection: "We're too busy to implement change."
Response: "Most of our clients say that. That's actually why WISE² exists—we handle the heavy lifting. Your team gets the benefit without the friction. We manage the transition."
Objection: "We're happy with what we have."
Response: "I hear you. The question isn't whether you're 'unhappy'—it's whether you're leaving money on the table. How many leads are you missing because you can't follow up? How many tech-savvy competitors are beating you? That's what we fix."
Objection: "We've tried software before and it didn't work."
Response: "That's actually really common. The difference with us is we don't sell you software and leave you. We implement it with you, train your team, and make sure it sticks. We're a partner, not a vendor."
Objection: "What's the timeline to ROI?"
Response: "Most of our NC clients see measurable ROI within 4-6 months. We often see the first savings (time saved = money saved) within 30 days. The Audit shows you exactly what you'll get."
