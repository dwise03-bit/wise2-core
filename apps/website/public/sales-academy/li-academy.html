Long Island Market Reality

Long Island businesses are practical. They don't want flashy — they want results. They're bootstrapped or modest-growth companies. They know their numbers. They're skeptical of vendors who oversell. They respect evidence and references more than promises.

Key Long Island Dynamics

  • Mid-tier deal values ($8K–$35K typical, vs $15K–$75K in NYC)
  • Longer sales cycles (60–90 days, due diligence matters)
  • One or two decision-makers (owner + operator)
  • Emphasis on ROI and payback period (not just annual value)
  • References matter—they know people, they'll call them

Long Island Industries We Serve

WISE² works with Nassau and Suffolk County businesses:

  • • HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical: Technician-dispatch companies that need scheduling, customer management, and dispatch optimization
  • • Construction & Contracting: GCs and subs managing crews, jobs, and subcontractor payments
  • • Retail & Local Businesses: Independent stores, restaurants, service shops needing POS + CRM + marketing
  • • Professional Services: Dental, medical, accounting practices modernizing patient/client management
  • • Family Businesses: Multi-generational operations ready to invest in systems and infrastructure
  • • Automotive & Repair: Auto shops, body shops, collision centers managing work orders and customers
  • • Real Estate & Property Mgmt: Local brokers and property managers scaling operations

Long Island Pricing Reality

Long Island customers are value-conscious but not cheap. They want to see the math. They'll invest if the ROI is clear.

Typical Long Island Deal Values

  • Audit: $3K–$5K (quick, focused assessment)
  • Launch: $8K–$15K (clean website + basic CRM)
  • Grow: $12K–$25K (marketing automation, lead nurture)
  • Automate: $20K–$50K (AI phone, workflows, integrations)
  • Dominate: $30K–$100K+ (custom, when business scales)
