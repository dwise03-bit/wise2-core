WISE² SALES CHEAT SHEET

Long Island Market • Print & Reference During Calls

📞 BEFORE EVERY CALL

  • Google them + look at their current website (you'll learn a lot)
  • Know their business basics (# of employees, type of work, rough revenue estimate)
  • Find a connection or reference if possible (they trust referrals)
  • Update CRM BEFORE you call
  • Have this cheat sheet + offer map at your desk

⏱️ Discovery Call Flow (45 min)

0-3 min: GREET
"Hi [name], this is [you] from WISE². I work with [industry] companies on [specific outcome]. Quick question: are you dealing with [specific problem] right now?"
3-20 min: AUDIT
"How many people on your team? How are you managing [key workflow]? What's costing you the most time or money right now?"
20-35 min: REVIEW
"What you just described—we see that all the time with [similar business]. Most end up spending [amount] to fix it. We helped [similar local company] solve it in [timeframe]."
35-45 min: CLOSE
"I think there's a fit here. We'd start with an Audit so you can make an informed decision. It's [price], takes [weeks]. Want to move forward?"

🎯 Long Island Elevator Pitches

15 sec: "We help local businesses stop wasting time on admin and systems. We save money and make operations run smoother."
30 sec: "We work with service companies and shops around Long Island. Most are spending 10+ hours per week on scheduling, paperwork, and follow-up. We automate all that."
60 sec: "I'm working with service companies and contractors around here. Like [recent similar local company]—they were losing customers to slow follow-up. We set them up with a system and their closing rate jumped. Happy to explore if there's something like that happening in your business."

🚧 Long Island Objection Responses

"That sounds expensive"
"Fair point. Here's the math: if your team spends X hours per week on [task], and you're at $Y per hour loaded cost, that's $[amount]/year. Our solution is $[lower amount]. You recoup it in the first [X] months."
"We're doing fine as is"
"That's great. Question: if you could free up 10 hours per week in your schedule and grow revenue 20%, would that interest you? Most business owners say yes to that math."
"We know another guy doing this"
"That's fair. What's he charging you? What's he actually done? [Listen]. We specialize in [specific outcome] for [specific business type]. We also come with [specific advantage]."
"Can you send me information?"
"I will. But here's what usually happens: you read it, it makes sense, then it sits. What if instead I send it, and we hop on a 15-min call [day/time] so I can answer questions?"
"I'll think about it"
"Good. What specifically do you want to think about? [Listen]. Let me address that right now. [Answer]. So—can we set up that Audit?"

⚡ Long Island Sales Rules

✓ Show the math: They want to see payback, not promises.
✗ Don't oversell: Start small, prove it, then grow.
✓ Use local references: They know people. References matter.
✗ Don't invent scope: Only promise what you can deliver. They talk.
✓ Listen first, pitch second: Understanding their business is 70% of the sale.
✗ Don't rush: Long Island deals take 60-90 days due diligence. That's OK.
