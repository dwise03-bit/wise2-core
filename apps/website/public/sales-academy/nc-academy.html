North Carolina Market Reality

NC contractors and service companies are serious operators. They value efficiency, know their metrics, and invest in what works. Growth is real but measured. They appreciate directness and results-driven approaches. References matter—they'll check.

Key NC Dynamics

  • Mid-tier deal values ($10K–$40K typical for HVAC/electrical/plumbing)
  • Sales cycles 45–75 days (thorough evaluation)
  • One to three decision-makers (usually owner + office manager)
  • ROI focus: payback period critical
  • Local references highly valued

North Carolina Industries We Serve

WISE² works with NC service companies and contractors:

  • • HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical: Technician dispatch, scheduling, job tracking, customer follow-up
  • • General Contractors & Subcontractors: Crew management, job coordination, payroll integration
  • • Roofing & Construction: Estimate generation, project timelines, supplier management
  • • Landscaping & Grounds: Service scheduling, crew tracking, customer management
  • • Automotive & Collision: Work orders, parts management, customer communication
  • • Local Service Businesses: Cleaning, maintenance, pest control, lawn care
  • • Professional Services: Accounting, legal, medical, dental practices

North Carolina Pricing Reality

NC businesses want clear ROI. They'll invest at the right price point when the numbers make sense.

Typical NC Deal Values

  • Audit: $3.5K–$6K (focused assessment)
  • Launch: $10K–$18K (clean systems setup)
  • Grow: $15K–$30K (marketing + automation)
  • Automate: $25K–$60K (AI, workflows, integrations)
  • Dominate: $40K–$120K+ (custom, when scaling)
