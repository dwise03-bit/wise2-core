## Manufacturer notes

- Confirm physical color swatches against the black/gold/red brand reference before sampling.
- Preserve the gold/red hierarchy and all copy verbatim.
- Keep minimum embroidered strokes and counters manufacturable; vendor may thicken fine strokes only when needed for stitch integrity.
- Confirm edge treatment, merrow/border profile, hook-loop dimensions, and final backing adhesive with the manufacturer.
- SVG masters are editable production starting points; approve one physical sample per construction type before bulk run.
