# Operating Rules

## Weekly 15-minute sync

Daniel and Darren review the index, filter for anything without a next action, and move new discoveries out of the inbox. The meeting is successful when both people can answer: what exists, who owns it, what can be sold or shown to a client, and what is blocked.

## Change rule

When a program changes status, owner, client scope, deployment location, or dependency, update its record and the index in the same session.

## Client gate

No program is labeled `client-ready` until it has an owner, deliverables, onboarding path, support owner, known limitations, and a verified deployment/demo path.

## Handoff rule

A handoff is complete only when the receiving person can find the record, run or view the program, understand current limitations, and identify the next action without asking the creator for missing context.
