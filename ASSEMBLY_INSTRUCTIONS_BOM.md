## ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS (USER MANUAL)

### TOOLS REQUIRED

**Minimum (essential)**:
- ✅ Hex key (4 mm, for M4 bolts)
- ✅ Hex key (2.5 mm, for M3 bolts)
- ✅ Screwdriver (Phillips, for quick-release caps)

**Optional (recommended)**:
- 🔨 Torque wrench (to 3 Nm for M4)
- 📐 Calipers (to verify fit)
- 🧹 Small brush (to clean aluminum dust)

---

### STEP 1: PREPARE WORKSTATION

**Time**: 5 minutes

1. **Clear workspace** (monitor stand needs ~60 cm length)
2. **Gather all parts** from print batches
3. **Verify all parts printed correctly**:
   - ✅ Left & Right feet (mirror images)
   - ✅ Crossbar adapters (identical)
   - ✅ Monitor holder (smooth, no defects)
   - ✅ Lock caps & clips present
4. **Cut aluminum crossbar** (if not pre-cut):
   - Measure: 300 mm total length
   - Cut 2 pieces: 150 mm each (leaves ~3 mm waste for saw)
   - Deburr edges: Use file or sandpaper
5. **Clean all aluminum** with dry cloth (remove manufacturing dust)

---

### STEP 2: INSTALL PACKOUT FEET

**Time**: 10 minutes | Difficulty: Easy

**What you're doing**: Attach printed feet to Milwaukee Packout lid

#### 2.1: Prepare Packout Lid
```
STEP 2.1.1: Ensure Packout is clean and dry
  ↓
STEP 2.1.2: Wipe lid with damp cloth, let dry completely
  ↓
STEP 2.1.3: Verify you can see the molded rib grid
  (Raised rectangular pattern across entire lid)
```

#### 2.2: Position Left Foot
```
STEP 2.2.1: Hold left foot with engagement ribs pointing UP
  (Ribs should face the same direction as Packout ribs)
  
STEP 2.2.2: Align with Packout left side
           ╱─ Left Foot ─╲
          │              │
  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
  │ ┌─────────────────────────────┤  ← Packout lid
  │ │ ╱╲╱╲╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱╱  │
  │ │ Molded ribs                  │
  └───────────────────────────────┘
  
STEP 2.2.3: Slide foot forward slowly
  (Engagement ribs should slide into Packout rib grooves)
  
STEP 2.2.4: Feel for positive CLICK
  (Indicates full engagement)
```

#### 2.3: Position Right Foot
```
STEP 2.3.1: Repeat Steps 2.2.1–2.2.4 for right foot
  (Mirror placement on right side of Packout)

STEP 2.3.2: Verify both feet are at same height
  (Use straight edge across top if needed)
```

#### 2.4: Secure with Lock Caps
```
STEP 2.4.1: Locate lock cap (small circular piece)

STEP 2.4.2: Align cap with center hole on foot
  (Keyed slot should point toward foot edge)

STEP 2.4.3: Press cap down and TURN 1/4 TURN clockwise
  (Should hear/feel second click = locked position)

STEP 2.4.4: Pull up gently on foot
  (Should NOT move if locked correctly)

STEP 2.4.5: Repeat for right lock cap
```

**Verification**:
- ✅ Both feet securely attached (no wobble)
- ✅ No visible gaps between foot and Packout
- ✅ Can remove foot by turning cap 1/4 turn & pulling up
- ✅ Packout lid shows ZERO damage (no cracks, indents)

### STEP 3: PREPARE ALUMINUM CROSSBAR

**Time**: 5 minutes | Difficulty: Easy

**What you're doing**: Clean & cut aluminum tube to size

```
STEP 3.1: Verify aluminum tube is straight
  ↓
STEP 3.2: Check for manufacturing residue/oil
  (Wipe with dry cloth if needed)
  ↓
STEP 3.3: Cut to 300 mm length (if not pre-cut)
  - Use hacksaw or chop saw
  - Mark with tape at 150 mm intervals
  - Cut two pieces: 150 mm each
  ↓
STEP 3.4: Deburr cut edges
  - Use file or coarse sandpaper
  - Smooth any sharp edges
  ↓
STEP 3.5: Clean away all dust
  - Wipe with damp cloth
  - Let dry completely
```

### STEP 4: ATTACH CROSSBAR ADAPTERS

**Time**: 15 minutes | Difficulty: Moderate

**What you're doing**: Mount aluminum tube to feet via printed clamps

```
STEP 4.1: Inspect crossbar adapters
  - Verify 4 M4 bolt holes are clear (not blocked by plastic)
  - Check that clamp opening is smooth (no rough edges)

STEP 4.2: Slide LEFT adapter onto aluminum tube
  - Insert tube into clamp opening
  - Verify tube slides smoothly (not too tight)
  - Position clamp so mounting tab points toward left foot

STEP 4.3: Slide RIGHT adapter onto other end
  - Same as Step 4.2 (mirror side)

STEP 4.4: Install M4 bolts (top & bottom)
  - Gather 4 × M4 bolts and 4 × T-slot nuts
  - Insert T-slot nut into M4 hole on adapter
  - Slide bolt through hole
  - Turn nut until hand-tight (NOT fully torqued yet)
  - Repeat for all 4 bolts (2 per adapter)
```

### STEP 5: BOLT ADAPTERS TO FEET

**Time**: 10 minutes | Difficulty: Moderate

**What you're doing**: Connect crossbar assembly to Packout feet

```
STEP 5.1: Position crossbar over feet
  - Align left adapter above left foot
  - Align right adapter above right foot
  - Verify crossbar is parallel to Packout lid (use level if needed)

STEP 5.2: Align mounting boss on foot with adapter tab
  - Look for M4 hole on foot (rear corner)
  - Position adapter tab over this hole
  - Adjust height so bolt hole aligns

STEP 5.3: Install first M4 bolt (left foot)
  - Insert bolt through adapter into foot
  - Thread bolt into hole hand-tight
  - Do NOT fully tighten yet

STEP 5.4: Repeat for right foot

STEP 5.5: Verify alignment
  - Check that crossbar is level (use level tool)
  - Check that feet are equally spaced
  - Adjust if needed before final torquing

STEP 5.6: Torque all bolts
  - Using 4 mm hex key:
    * Torque to 3 Nm (or hand-tight + 1/4 turn)
    * DO NOT over-tighten (risk of stripping plastic)
  - Go around in star pattern: tighten all 4 bolts equally
```

**Torque Reference**:
```
Hand-tight = ~0.5 Nm (just stop when snug)
Our target  = 3.0 Nm (hand-tight + noticeable resistance)
Over-tightened = Bolt resists turning (STOP)
```

**Verification**:
- ✅ Crossbar doesn't rock or shift
- ✅ Feet remain securely on Packout
- ✅ All bolts are snug (no rattle)
- ✅ Crossbar is level & horizontal

### STEP 6: ATTACH MONITOR HOLDER

**Time**: 10 minutes | Difficulty: Moderate

**What you're doing**: Mount the monitor slot assembly to crossbar

```
STEP 6.1: Inspect monitor holder
  - Verify monitor slot is smooth (no burrs)
  - Check mounting holes are clear
  - Ensure cable routing openings are accessible

STEP 6.2: Position holder on crossbar
  - Place holder perpendicular to crossbar
  - Center holder along crossbar length
  - Verify 20° angle faces user (monitor viewing angle)

STEP 6.3: Align mounting holes
  - Locate M4 bolt holes on monitor holder (2 points)
  - Align with crossbar holes
  - Use plastic shims if needed to level

STEP 6.4: Install M4 bolts
  - Insert bolts through holder into crossbar T-nuts
  - Start both bolts hand-tight
  - Verify holder is level

STEP 6.5: Torque bolts
  - Torque to 3 Nm (4 mm hex key)
  - Alternate between left & right bolts
```

### STEP 7: INSTALL TPU PADS

**Time**: 5 minutes | Difficulty: Easy

**What you're doing**: Add protective cushion to monitor slot

```
STEP 7.1: Prepare TPU pads
  - Have 4 pads ready (10×10×3 mm each)
  - Verify adhesive backing is present (3M VHB)

STEP 7.2: Peel adhesive backing
  - Start one corner of pad
  - Slowly peel release liner (don't touch adhesive)

STEP 7.3: Position pads in slot corners
  - 2 pads in upper corners of slot
  - 2 pads where crossbar meets holder
  - Press firmly (20 seconds per pad for adhesive cure)

STEP 7.4: Allow adhesive to cure
  - Wait 24 hours before loading monitor
  - (VHB tape strength increases over time)
```

## DISASSEMBLY & MAINTENANCE

### Disassembly (Reverse Order)

```
STEP 1: Disconnect monitor cables
STEP 2: Gently pull monitor out of slot
STEP 3: Remove cable clip bolts (M3)
STEP 4: Unbolt monitor holder from crossbar (M4)
STEP 5: Unbolt crossbar from feet (M4)
STEP 6: Remove aluminum crossbar & adapters
STEP 7: Release lock caps (turn 1/4 turn CCW)
STEP 8: Lift feet straight up to disengage ribs
```

### Maintenance Schedule

| Interval | Task | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| After each use | Wipe monitor & holder | Dry cloth only |
| Weekly | Check all bolt tightness | Look for rattle |
| Monthly | Inspect TPU pads | Should stick firmly |
| Annually | Replace TPU pads (if worn) | Protect new monitor better |
| As needed | Touch up WISE² logo | Small paint pen if faded |

### Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Monitor shifts | Bolt loose | Re-torque M4 bolts to 3 Nm |
| Foot slips on Packout | Rib engagement worn | Replace foot (reprint Part A-001/A-002) |
| Cable clips rattle | M3 bolts loose | Tighten with 2.5 mm hex key |
| TPU pads peeling | Adhesive failed | Clean slot, apply new pads (3M VHB) |
| Packout shows marks | Foot rubbing | Add rubber feet underneath (optional) |
| Monitor wobbles | Structural flex | Verify crossbar is level, check all bolts |

## STORAGE & TRANSPORT

### Transport Case Packing

```
1. Disconnect all cables
2. Remove monitor (if not needed)
3. Unbolt monitor holder from crossbar
4. Remove aluminum crossbar
5. Release lock caps & lift feet off Packout
6. Pack in foam-lined case:
   - Packout with feet removed (at bottom)
   - Monitor holder (wrapped)
   - Aluminum crossbar (wrapped)
   - Feet (4 pieces, bag together)
   - Cables (coiled, separate pouch)
   - Bolts/hardware (labeled ziplock)
   - This manual (for reference)
```

### Shelf Storage

- Store in cool, dry place (not damp/humid)
- Keep away from direct sunlight (UV degrades PLA)
- Store components unassembled (prevent stress on joints)
- Check annually for PLA brittleness (flex test)
