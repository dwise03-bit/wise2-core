## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account & Profile Information**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Business/company affiliation
- Profile photo (optional)
- Technician ID or credentials

**Job & Service Data**
- Customer names and contact information
- Customer addresses and locations
- Equipment details (model, serial numbers, specifications)
- Service call descriptions and complaint summaries
- Job status and scheduling information
- Pricing quotes and service recommendations

**Technical & Measurement Data**
- HVAC equipment readings (pressures, temperatures, superheat, subcooling, CFM)
- Fieldpiece device measurements and diagnostic data
- Equipment serial numbers and nameplate information
- Electrical readings and calculations

**Documentation & Content**
- Photographs of equipment, job sites, repairs, and before/after images
- Service notes and observations (text, voice notes)
- Saved manuals, wiring diagrams, and troubleshooting guides
- Generated service reports and documentation

**Communications**
- Customer history and previous communications
- In-app messages with WISE² IMP assistant
- Support tickets and feedback

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information**
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Device identifiers (e.g., Android ID)
- Device settings and configuration

**Usage Analytics**
- Features used and frequency of use
- Pages/screens viewed and time spent
- User interactions and navigation patterns
- App crashes and performance metrics

**Location Data**
- GPS coordinates (when you enable location services)
- Address data for job dispatch and navigation
- Used for: GPS routing, technician location tracking, job assignment

**Camera & Microphone Data**
- Photos captured within the App (equipment, job sites)
- Audio recordings of voice notes (stored locally or in cloud)
- Not transmitted unless you explicitly save/sync

**Fieldpiece Device Data**
- Data streamed from connected Fieldpiece tools
- Bluetooth connection information
- Real-time measurements and readings

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal data based on:

- **Account data**: Retained while account is active + 30 days after deletion
- **Job/service records**: Retained for 7 years (industry standard for HVAC service records)
- **Photos & documentation**: Retained as long as associated job is retained
- **Analytics/logs**: Retained for up to 1 year
- **Usage data**: Retained for up to 90 days

**User Deletion Rights**: You can request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements) by emailing privacy@wisedefensellc.com.

## 7. Data Security

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS/SSL encryption for all data transmitted to/from servers
