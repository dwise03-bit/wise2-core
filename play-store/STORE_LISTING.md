## Full Description (4,000 characters max)

### Primary Positioning

WISE² HVAC Field Agent is the AI-powered command center for HVAC technicians. Complete jobs faster, diagnose accurately, and capture professional reports—all from your phone or tablet, online or offline.

### Key Features

**Field Job Management**
- View today's service calls with customer details and equipment info
- Track job status from assignment through completion
- Add technician notes and photos on-site
- Offline access to all job details when connectivity is limited

**HVAC Diagnostics & Calculators**
- Interactive troubleshooting workflows for common failure modes (no cooling, no heating, electrical faults, etc.)
- Professional HVAC calculators: superheat, subcooling, temperature split, voltage/current imbalance
- AI-assisted diagnostic analysis to suggest next testing steps
- Capture measurements (pressures, temperatures, voltage) from connected instruments

**Connected Equipment Profiles**
- Full equipment specs: tonnage, refrigerant type, voltage, phase, filter size
- Service history tracking
- Equipment photos and notes
- One-click access to prior repairs and recommendations

**IMP AI Assistance**
- Real-time AI technician support—ask questions about diagnostics, equipment, troubleshooting
- AI learns your job context (current equipment, measurements, complaint) for smarter guidance
- Structured recommendations to keep diagnostics on track

**Professional Service Reports**
- Generate polished service reports in minutes
- Auto-populate customer, equipment, and job details
- Structured sections for findings, work performed, parts used, recommendations
- PDF generation ready for customer handoff

**Offline-First Field Operations**
- Full offline mode when connectivity is unavailable
- Automatic sync when you reconnect
- Never lose a note, measurement, or photo
- Sync status always visible

**Connected Instruments**
- Ready for Fieldpiece, Fluke, and other HVAC tools
- Extensible architecture for new instrument adapters
- Simulated readings for training and testing

### Who It's For

HVAC field technicians, service managers, and contractor shops that need:
- Faster diagnostics on the jobsite
- Professional documentation
- Field data capture that syncs to the cloud
- AI-assisted troubleshooting

### Why WISE² Field Agent

- **Purpose-built for HVAC** — Not a generic note app; understands HVAC workflows, equipment, diagnostics
