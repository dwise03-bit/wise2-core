## Permissions Justification

For the app privacy policy / permissions section:

- **Camera** — Capture equipment photos, before/after documentation, nameplate scanning
- **Microphone** — Voice notes during fieldwork, WISE² IMP voice interaction and commands
- **Location (GPS)** — GPS dispatch routing, technician location sharing, job site navigation
- **Contacts** — Customer information management, call history, customer history
- **Files/Storage** — Access saved documentation, manuals, reports, equipment knowledge base
- **Bluetooth** — Fieldpiece tool integration (compatible pressure/temperature probes, manifolds, smart tools)
- **Internet** — Cloud sync, AI diagnostics, real-time Fieldpiece data, navigation, knowledge base updates
