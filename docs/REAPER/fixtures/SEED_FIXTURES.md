# REAPER V1 — Seed Fixture Definitions

## SEED-001 GOLDEN PROSPECT
Strong business, excellent reviews, weak website, weak conversion, moderate brand.
Expected: very high opportunity.

## SEED-002 STRONG EVERYTHING
Strong business, website, brand, reputation.
Expected: strong health, low/medium opportunity.

## SEED-003 WEAK BUSINESS / WEAK DIGITAL
Poor public signals, poor reputation, poor website.
Expected: low opportunity despite digital weakness.

## SEED-004 INCOMPLETE DATA
Website available, social unavailable, reviews incomplete.
Expected: UNKNOWN modules, lower confidence, no false zero.

## SEED-005 STRONG REVIEWS / OUTDATED WEBSITE
Expected: Website Transformation pattern.

## SEED-006 VISUAL SERVICE / NO VIDEO
Expected: Short-Form Video opportunity.

## SEED-007 SAME NAME COLLISION
Two businesses share a name but differ in city/phone/domain.
Expected: no merge.

## SEED-008 CLIENT IMPROVEMENT
Before:
Website 42
Conversion 35
Digital Execution 44

After:

Expected: Opportunity Score declines while success delta is visible.
