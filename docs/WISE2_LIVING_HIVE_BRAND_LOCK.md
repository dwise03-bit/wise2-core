# WISE² LIVING HIVE — GLOBAL BRAND IDENTITY LOCK

Status: CANONICAL / GLOBAL
Effective: 2026-10-06
Scope: WISE² parent brand and all first-party WISE² product surfaces.
Brand Lock Version: 2.0 — Living Hive

## Master Brand Statement
**WISE² — The AI Business Operating System**

**Tell us what needs to happen.**

WISE² is not positioned as a chatbot, generic AI assistant, or collection of disconnected dashboards. It is a living business operating system that understands a requested outcome, assembles the right intelligence, verifies the work, keeps the human in control, executes through connected tools and devices, and compounds the result into reusable organizational knowledge.

## Positioning
WISE² is **The AI Business Operating System**.
Primary interaction promise: **Tell us what needs to happen.**
WISE² turns natural-language requests into researched, approved, executed, stored business outcomes.

## Canonical Operating Story
REQUEST → WISE COMMAND / HERMES → OWL EYE + SPECIALIZED HIVE AGENTS → APPROVAL CHECKPOINT → EXECUTION → RESULTS + SAUCE VAULT

This flow is a product, UX, marketing, and storytelling primitive. Show work happening rather than merely describing AI.

## Living Hive Visual Identity
The signature WISE² metaphor is a living, connected intelligence system.
- Premium cinematic realism and dimensional depth.
- Black / deep navy foundation.
- Chrome / metallic WISE² and W² identity.
- Restrained electric blue and violet signaling.
- Warm energy may appear as purposeful operational illumination, but do not turn the global identity into orange cartoon styling.
- Avoid generic neon-cyberpunk dashboard treatment as the default.
- Avoid cute/cartoon agents as the primary corporate expression.
- Interfaces should feel alive: routes illuminate, agents activate, nodes respond, approvals interrupt execution, completed knowledge returns to Sauce Vault.
- The W² core is the visual anchor: intelligence and activity radiate from it rather than competing with it.
- Motion must communicate system state, routing, confidence, approval or execution—not decorative noise.
- Dense operational information belongs in secondary surfaces; hero experiences prioritize comprehension, depth and a single obvious request path.

## Intelligence Architecture
- **WISE Command / Hermes** — command, intent understanding, orchestration, routing, progress.
- **Owl Eye** — research, verification, evidence and confidence.
- **Specialized Hive Agents** — sales, web, creative, operations, finance and additional domain agents.
- **Shannon** — authorized security specialist. Requires explicit target, scope and approval before scans/tests or consequential security actions. Never present autonomous unauthorized security activity.
- **Sauce Vault** — persistent results, reusable knowledge, evidence and organizational memory.
- **Hive Nodes** — Surface, VPS, Razr, Pi and future approved WISE² devices/services.

Agents are specialists inside one WISE² intelligence system—not separate mascots competing for brand ownership. Their identities may be recognizable, but WISE Command and the W² core remain visually and operationally dominant.

## Human Control
Approval checkpoints are a visible part of the brand and product promise.
Research and preparation may precede approval. Consequential external actions require appropriate approval/consent. Security actions use stricter Shannon gates.

Canonical trust language: **Research + verify before action. Approval before consequence. Results reported back.**

## Experience Principles
1. **Conversation is the interface.** A person should be able to state an outcome in natural language.
2. **Show the work.** Surface thinking state, research, routing, confidence, approvals, execution and results without exposing private chain-of-thought.
3. **One Hive, many specialists.** Route work to the best agent rather than presenting one bot as capable of everything.
4. **Human control is visible.** Approval is a product feature, not hidden compliance text.
5. **API first.** Prefer native/API integrations; use browser/computer control as a fallback when appropriate.
6. **Persistent intelligence.** Completed work should become reusable context in Sauce Vault where appropriate.
7. **Real outcomes over demos.** Brand claims should map to functioning capabilities or clearly labeled concepts.

## Core Messaging Vocabulary
Prefer: **WISE Command, Living Hive, Hive Agents, Owl Eye, Shannon, Approval Checkpoint, Execution, Hive Nodes, Sauce Vault, outcome, verify, approve, execute, compound.**

Avoid making **AI chatbot, AI bot, cyberpunk dashboard, autonomous everything, magic AI** the primary description of WISE².

Primary CTA pattern: **Tell WISE² what needs to happen.**
Supporting CTA patterns may include: **Put WISE² to work**, **Review & approve**, **See the Hive at work**, and outcome-specific commands.

## Brand Separation Rule
This global lock does **not** erase established sub-brand identities or client brands.
Wise Defense, WISE² Trading, Sound Lab, Wise Shine, client projects and other locked brands retain their approved palettes, logos, likenesses and domain-specific visual systems. They may inherit WISE² orchestration language/patterns where appropriate, but must not be blindly recolored or visually homogenized.

## Identity Preservation
Preserve canonical WISE² marks and locked human likeness references. Do not substitute identities, recolor locked logos, or invent replacement marks when approved assets exist.

## Implementation Rule
For new or materially redesigned first-party WISE² UI:
REFERENCE → INSPECT → MEASURE → BUILD → RUN → CAPTURE → COMPARE → CORRECT → REPEAT → VERIFY.

When an older WISE² brand document conflicts with this lock for the parent/first-party brand, **this document wins**. Product-specific locked identity rules still win within their explicitly scoped sub-brand/product.

## Success Test
A WISE² experience should communicate:
1. Tell WISE² what needs to happen.
2. WISE Command understands and routes it.
3. Owl Eye and specialized intelligence research, verify and prepare the work.
4. The human remains in control at approval points.
5. WISE² executes through the best available integration/control path.
6. Results and reusable knowledge return to Sauce Vault.
7. The system visibly learns and compounds rather than behaving like a static dashboard.
