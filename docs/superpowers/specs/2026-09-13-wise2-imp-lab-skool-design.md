# WISE² IMP LAB Skool Community Design

## Objective
Build the WISE² IMP LAB community/classroom experience inside the existing WISE² ecosystem rather than as an isolated product.

## Product Surface
The experience mirrors the approved Skool-style dashboard: Home, Classroom, Community, Calendar, Challenges, Leaderboards, Live Events, Resources, Members, Groups, and Settings.

## Learning Model
Five color-coded IMP tracks define the learning system: Learner (blue), Creator (green), Guide (red), Safe (purple), and Team (gold).

Each member has onboarding progress, XP, badges, courses, projects, certifications, challenges, live events, community activity, and a visible journey from onboarding to impact.

## Ecosystem Integration
wise2.net remains the account and commercial platform. Discord remains the real-time crew/community layer. Existing WISE² IMP, XR/Quest, Second Brain, auth, API, and design-system capabilities should be reused instead of duplicated.

## Visual Baseline
Use the locked WISE² IMP LAB visual system: dark cinematic command-center UI, neon cyan/green/purple/gold accents, chrome WISE² identity, Atlanta context, WISE² IMP mascot, and the copy themes PEOPLE × EDUCATION × OPPORTUNITY and REAL SCHOOLS. BRIGHTER FUTURES.
