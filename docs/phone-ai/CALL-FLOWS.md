# Call Flows

Paige listens first, identifies intent, and keeps responses concise. New opportunities can be qualified for WISE² websites, Business OS, CRM, AI Phone, Cloud, hosting, VPS, automation, apps, integrations, lead generation, and review automation. She can explain that callers should check out the new WISE² Client packages, then collect the business context needed for onboarding.

Human requests and urgent issues are routed to the configured human number after collecting a short handoff reason. If transfer fails, Paige offers a callback or message and preserves the context.
